Yes. You need to login or register before adding products to your cart or wishlist.

Products are added from the Django admin panel using category, subcategory, images, sizes, and stock.

Yes. Use the heart icon to add products to your wishlist.

Yes. Logged-in users can rate and review products from the product detail page.
